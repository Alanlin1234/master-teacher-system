# Frontend Taste Skill (Taste-Skill)

## 1. The Three Design Dials
Tune these settings to calibrate the personality of the UI:
- **DESIGN_VARIANCE (Target: 7/10)**:
  - Break rigid 3-column bento grids.
  - Employ intentional asymmetric balance (e.g., 60/40 hero layout, editorial callouts, staggered cards).
- **MOTION_INTENSITY (Target: 6/10)**:
  - Physics-informed micro-interactions: Smooth state transitions, hover lift, organic audio-lip sync for avatars.
  - Purposeful entrance choreography (staggered fade-up with small offsets: 12px-20px).
- **VISUAL_DENSITY (Target: 6/10)**:
  - Generous whitespace around primary focus elements; compact, data-dense cards for telemetry and radar scores.
  - Clear hierarchy: Bold statement -> Supporting narrative -> Actionable toolbars.

## 2. Refusal of AI Tells
- **Typography with Personality**: Pair modern sans-serifs for interface precision with warm humanist touches for pedagogical quotes.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Soft ambient glows (`radial-gradient` with low opacity `0.08~0.15`), textured borders, fine frosted surfaces.
- **Educational Charm**: Clean icons, tactile buttons, real-time emotion feedback for digital humans.
